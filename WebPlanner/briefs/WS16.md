## Tasks in order (gate targets)

1. **Spike (≤ 1 h, V0):** canvas-source field layer at field resolution on the integrated-GPU laptop. Measure frame rate while updating at 30 Hz flat and at 60° tilt with terrain. Decide: canvas source everywhere, or a custom map layer in 2D plus canvas source at a lower rate in 3D. Write the result to `STATUS.md`.
2. **Field shader, bands mode (V1):** nearest sampling, manual bilinear on decoded values, unknown if any neighbour unknown; bands for the three variants; hatch; polygon fallback without WebGL2. Expose `fieldAt(lon, lat)` for WS15 and WS18.
3. **Margin mode (V1):** uniforms from the mission state; the formula in DESIGN §U2, O₂ term in minutes at 1.35 L/min, mirroring the WS2 margin code; zero edge line; binding-limit label at the edge.
4. **Collapse (V1):** `u_k` blend of flat time (distance / 0.98 m/s) and `T_home`; 3 s; headline count from the manifest; reduced-motion cross-fade.
5. **Thread home, route draw-on, markers, furniture (V1):** follower over the pointer field, dash flow, tooltip; route draw-on; markers with value rings and state fills; compass with sun direction; cursor readout. Hand the path function to WS17; the ghost-route layer to WS18.
6. **Labels (V1):** `labels.py` → `labels.json` (DESIGN §6.2); symbol layers with collision, sort key, zoom ranges; stretchable pill icon; selected layer ignoring collisions; hover expansion.
7. **Terrain exports (V2):** `terrain_tiles.py` (Terrarium, elevation × 1.878, z10–15 planning extent, z16 focus area, metadata with the factor and source hash, ≤ 25 MB); `relief_lowzoom.py` (z6–10 over Gale, ≤ 5 MB). Manifest entries for both.
8. **3D terrain in the planning map (V2):** terrain source, separate relief-shading source, sun-driven shading direction, exaggeration slider with visible factor, 2D/3D toggle, flat fallback below 30 fps; leader lines above 30° tilt.
9. **Camera presets and focus transitions (V3):** keys 1–5 (DESIGN §U16).
10. **Flythrough and storyboards (V3):** smoothed route camera; director with the storyboard schema; `intro.json`, `flythrough.json`, `storm.json`, `kiosk.json`, `video_beat1.json`; `?demo=1`, `?capture=…`.
11. **Overlays and highlights (V3):** contours, slope classes, rough footing, mineral hints, ridges, blocked areas, data quality; highlight polygons for selection; layer manager rows and legends (with WS15 components).
12. **Storm mode (V3):** dose curve hook for the timeline, leash banner, dose ring, thread colour, timing note.
13. **Bookmarks, isolation, focus mode, minimap, selection history (Tier B rest).**
14. **Capture (Sun 12:00–14:00):** run the video storyboards at 1920 × 1080.

## Acceptance tests (write first)

- Shader margin equals `timeline_*.json` within 0.5 min at every station and every 5 min of the route.
- With the precomputed band inputs, the 30-min edge lies within one field cell (5 m) of the contour.
- Unknown cells are never coloured; 2D and 3D pictures are identical.
- Collapse: k = 0 gives a 1,764 m circle within one cell; k = 1 equals the band; counted headline equals the manifest.
- Thread time equals `T_home` at its start within 0.5 min; never crosses blocked or unknown cells.
- Terrain: a reference ridge's height-to-width ratio at 1× matches the 1 m model within 5%; layers stay registered at all tilts.
- Labels: no overlap on the six key screens at five zooms; route stations always visible.
- Storyboards: two capture runs give identical frames.
- Storm: tripwire and leash equal `rad.json`; the timing note is always present.
- Frame rate: 60 fps in 2D while scrubbing, ≥ 30 fps at 60° tilt on the integrated-GPU laptop.

## Don't

Filter encoded textures; read elevations for display from the scaled tiles; draw unknown terrain in a warning colour; add effects that are not in DESIGN; quote any number that is not in the manifest or a field.

## Fallback

Shader too slow: polygons with margin colouring by station, plus the thread and labels. Terrain too heavy: relief shading only, flat map.
