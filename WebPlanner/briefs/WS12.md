## Known-answer tests

- User A cannot read, change or delete user B's plan (API test and a direct RLS test).
- A guest cannot list plans; a share code resolves without login; a private plan's id does not.
- The seventh heavy guest job in 10 minutes is refused with a retry time.
- Oversized or malformed uploads are refused; unknown fields are rejected.
- CORS refuses an origin outside the allow-list.
- The deploy script stops when the parity test fails, and after the deadline.
