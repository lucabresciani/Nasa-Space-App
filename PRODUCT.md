# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

unity-desktop (Unity 6.6, URP, Windows standalone; first-person; not a web surface)

## Users

Astronauts on a Mars EVA. The in-game interface is their helmet HUD: while walking the surface they look at a rock, scan it, and read what it is made of without stopping the traverse.

## Product Purpose

A NASA Space Apps project: a walkable, procedurally generated Mars landscape (2 km terrain, craters, ~1,200 boulders) in which every rock carries scientific data. The rock scanner turns a glance at a boulder into a geochemical reading: composition, mineralogy, spectrum and physical properties. Success means the astronaut can identify a rock class and decide whether it is worth sampling in a few seconds.

## Positioning

Readings are grounded in real public NASA rover measurements from the Mount Sharp region of Gale crater (Curiosity APXS chemistry of drilled and brushed rock, CheMin mineralogy, ChemCam meteorite data) instead of invented numbers; each in-game rock is an analog of a real measured Mount Sharp target. Sources are listed in DATA_SOURCES.md.

## Operating Context

- First-person traverse on foot in Mars gravity; keyboard/mouse and gamepad.
- Bright, dusty, orange daylight scene behind the HUD; the HUD must stay legible over sunlit terrain and sky.
- The HUD shares the visor with the view: it must not block the path ahead while walking.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Rock data must come from public NASA data. The exact source format is **undecided**; the data layer must be easy to edit and to re-import when the format is chosen.
- UI language: English.
- Rocks are generated by `Tools/Mars/Build Mars Landscape Scene`; science data must survive regeneration.

## Evidence on Hand

- Rock data: Mount Sharp analog database (`Assets/Mars/Resources/MarsRockDatabase.json`, 32 Curiosity analyses from the PDS MSL APXS supplements). Values are analogs of real measured targets, cited per sample, never measurements of the in-game rocks. Class physical/spectral properties are proxies or unknown where not measured on the unit.

## Product Principles

1. Glanceable first, deep on demand: the class and the one decisive number before the full table.
2. Never fabricate science: every value traces to a cited measurement or is labeled as a derived estimate.
3. The view ahead stays clear: the HUD yields the center of the visor while moving.
4. Data is swappable: changing the dataset never requires touching scene or UI code.
